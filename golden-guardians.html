GSW Golden Guardians Rebrand

Year
2019
Client
Golden State Warriors
Creative Direction
Crystal Au, Will Dove
Design & Art
Will Dove (Designer), Cindy Hoang (Mascot Illustration), Sarah Brown (Environmental)

Rebrand

The rebrand and visual development for Golden Guardians was intended to help brand recognition in the esports and gaming space while still retaining its roots and ties to the Golden State Warriors.

Mascot Development

To strengthen the visual identity and presence of Golden Guardians' new identity, we leaned into the creation of a Guardian mascot that acted as key art and another visual tool to represent the team's story.

Merch Design

The launch also introduced the refreshed identity through various merchandise items, jerseys, and apparel.

Patterns

Custom patterns were created to maintain a cohesive overarching look while giving different games a unique visual element to accompany social and gameday graphics.
