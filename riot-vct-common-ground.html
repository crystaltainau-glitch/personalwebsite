VCT Common Ground Donut Shop (2024)

I developed the branding and visual identity for the Common Ground donut shop. We decorated Fonuts in Los Angeles with branded boxes, cups, donut gun buddy keychains, donut placards, and posters. The donut shop was an integral part of pro VALORANT player Jawgemo's background.

VCT Common Ground Tattoo Shop (2026)

The 2026 episode of Common Ground features a set and illustrations inspired by traditional tattoo art. The illustrations pull from VALORANT imagery and elements from the players and host's backgrounds. All flash tattoos were wrapped up into a custom Common Ground flash book.
